India's Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri revealed that 51 out of 227 Indian nationals recruited into the Russian armed forces have died. Misri shared the figure in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, noting that the issue remains an active concern for New Delhi.

Most of the affected men were reportedly lured by agents promising support roles like security guards or logistics positions, but were sent to front-line duty instead. Authorities previously stated that recruits were misled about contracts, and some had their passports withheld upon arrival.

The Indian government continues to press Moscow for the release and repatriation of those still serving. Officials have periodically issued advisories warning citizens against unofficial recruitment agents offering high-paying jobs in Russia, cautioning that the offers can be a front for military conscription.
