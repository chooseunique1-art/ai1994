Iran launched missile and drone strikes toward Kuwait on September 3, 2026, as retaliation for recent American military strikes in the region. Sirens sounded as air defense systems intercepted the incoming attacks, and the Kuwaiti Defence Ministry called the action blatant Iranian aggression on social media. Officials stated that explosions heard in the capital were caused by air defenses intercepting hostile targets rather than direct impacts, and they assured residents that the situation was under control.

The attack followed Bahrain and Jordan reporting that their air defenses intercepted several Iranian attacks the previous day. This escalation stems from earlier hostilities after American forces struck Iranian rocket launchers on an island in the Strait of Hormuz, prompting Iran to target Gulf states that host American military infrastructure. 

The 2026 war began in February between the United States, Israel, and Iran, and although a ceasefire took hold in June, President Trump declared the war over in July following renewed Iranian attacks on shipping. Tensions have since remained elevated with the Strait of Hormuz acting as a flashpoint. US officials stated that all options remain on the table without providing a timeline for the conflict's end, leaving Kuwait exposed to the fallout despite not being a direct combatant.
