US President Donald Trump stated on Friday that he will halt trade with countries where the United States has a trade deficit, unless the Federal Reserve lowers interest rates. Writing on his Truth Social platform, Trump claimed that high interest rates place the United States at an unfair global disadvantage. He repeated his frequent demand that the nation should have the lowest interest rate in the world.

The threat was made on the same day the Bureau of Labor Statistics released stronger-than-expected August job creation figures, prompting traders to raise expectations for a Federal Reserve interest rate move in September. Trump has repeatedly clashed with the independent central bank, arguing that lower borrowing costs are necessary to support growth and reduce the government's debt-servicing burden. 

Economists have questioned the practicality of linking monetary policy decisions to executive trade policy. It remains unclear if the administration plans to act on the trade threat or is merely using it as pressure ahead of the central bank's upcoming policy meeting.
