Finnish authorities launched an investigation on September 1, 2026, after a member of the public discovered an unidentified fixed-wing drone washed ashore in the Pellinki archipelago near the town of Porvoo. A Border Guard patrol confirmed the find and began examining how the aircraft arrived on Finnish territory, looking at its likely flight path or drifting route across the Gulf of Finland. 

Preliminary assessments indicate the aircraft is a Geoscan 701 model typically used for aerial surveying and mapping rather than military or explosive purposes, posing no immediate danger to the public. However, the Finnish Ministry of Defence described the discovery as a possible territorial violation by an unmanned aerial vehicle, opening the door to a formal investigation into whether Finnish sovereignty was breached.

Investigators have not yet established the drone's country of origin, and officials cautioned it is too early to determine whether it came from Russia, which lies just across the gulf. Finland has reported several stray drones crossing into its territory over the past year, including Ukrainian drones that drifted off course amid the war in eastern Europe, adding to a pattern of incidents that have unsettled NATO member states bordering Russia.
