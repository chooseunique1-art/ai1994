At the 2026 Asian Games, the Indian badminton pair of Satwik and Chirag managed to overcome the reigning world champions.  However, this individual success was not enough to elevate the entire Indian men's team to a medal position beyond bronze.

Their overall standing was ultimately determined by a defeat against the Chinese squad.  As a direct result of that loss, the Indian men's contingent had to settle for a bronze medal.

The outcome underscores how a single crucial defeat can overshadow a notable victory within a team competition.
