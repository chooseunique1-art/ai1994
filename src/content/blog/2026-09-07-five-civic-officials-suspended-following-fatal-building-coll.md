Municipal authorities in Delhi suspended five civic officials—including South Zone Deputy Commissioner Rakesh Kumar and Superintending Engineer Ranvir Singh, alongside executive engineers and building inspectors from the local ward—following the weekend collapse of a five-storey residential building in Satya Niketan. The disaster resulted in seven deaths and several injuries. Preliminary investigations showed that unauthorized structural modifications and compromised foundational integrity caused the failure.

Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta visited the site to review search and rescue efforts by disaster response teams using specialized radar and heavy machinery to clear concrete debris. The Chief Minister ordered a comprehensive magisterial probe to fix criminal and administrative liability, while the Delhi High Court directed a high-level inquiry into municipal oversight and building safety standards. 

Local law enforcement registered a formal First Information Report against the building owner for negligence and illegal construction practices. Following the tragedy, municipal authorities announced an emergency sealing drive targeting hazardous structures across the south and southwest Delhi districts.
