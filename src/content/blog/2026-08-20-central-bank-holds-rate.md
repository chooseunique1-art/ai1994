Policymakers voted 7-2 to keep the benchmark rate unchanged, citing persistent
services inflation despite cooling goods prices.

The committee's statement flagged energy costs as an upside risk heading into
the winter months, a shift in language from the prior meeting.

Traders had priced in a small chance of a cut; futures markets showed little
reaction to the decision.
