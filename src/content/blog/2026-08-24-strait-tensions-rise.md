Shipping insurers raised war-risk premiums on routes through the Strait of Hormuz
this week after naval patrols in the area increased noticeably.

Several tanker operators confirmed rerouting or delaying transits while they
wait for clearer guidance from maritime authorities.

Analysts caution that the increase mirrors past periods of heightened rhetoric
rather than any confirmed incident, but note that premiums remain a leading
indicator markets watch closely.
