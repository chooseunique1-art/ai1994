On August 14, 2026, the OSINT account MenchOsint published flight-tracking data and images alleging that Israeli army soldiers and officers arrived in Colombia on a Boeing 707 "Re'em" tanker aircraft. According to the post, the aircraft flew from the Middle East with its transponder switched off and identification markings removed. 

In a separate quote-tweet, the same account claimed that Morocco permitted the Israeli aircraft to fly through its airspace on the way to the United States after Spain refused the request. Officials from Israel, Colombia, and Morocco have not confirmed either claim, and mainstream outlets have not independently corroborated the flight.
