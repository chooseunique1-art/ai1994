Korea defeated India 3-1 in the Davis Cup, ending India's historic Final 8 hopes. The result was sealed after Sumit Nagal lost 1-6 2-6 to Korea number one Soon Woo Kwon in the must-win rubber during difficult heat conditions. 

Earlier, Dhakshineswar Suresh and N. Sriram Balaji had revived India's hopes by securing a fighting doubles victory to cut the deficit to 1-2. 

The developments have drawn attention from observers, stakeholders, and the general public, with official spokespersons highlighting the need to adhere to established protocols as administrative reviews continue.
