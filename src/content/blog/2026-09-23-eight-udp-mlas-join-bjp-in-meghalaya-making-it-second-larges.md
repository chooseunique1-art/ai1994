Eight United Democratic Party legislators officially joined the Bharatiya Janata Party at the party's national headquarters in New Delhi on Wednesday. The group includes former state ministers and prominent leaders. With these eight additions and prior members, the BJP's total strength in the Meghalaya State Assembly rose to ten, making it the second-largest party behind the ruling National People's Party.

The move followed months of discussions regarding state governance, development projects, and political strategy, with the incoming legislators submitting demands such as the inclusion of the Khasi language in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution. Party officials expressed confidence that the realignment will accelerate infrastructure development and central policy implementation. 

Political analysts suggest the expansion will reshape coalition dynamics within the ruling alliance ahead of the 2028 assembly polls, while regional opposition groups expressed surprise and plan to reorganize local constituency frameworks. Cabinet leadership and floor management in Shillong are expected to adjust committee appointments following the official notification.
