On August 15, 2026, a video shared by UAE_BARQ and credited to TRT Haber showed a shopping mall ceiling collapsing in China. The collapse occurred after rainwater leaked through the roof, sending water and debris crashing down near an interior walkway. 

According to the video's caption, the mall was closed and work in the affected area was halted while repairs are assessed. No casualties were reported from the incident. 

The exact city and the name of the mall were not specified in the post or visible in the video captions. The extent of the structural damage remains unconfirmed beyond what was shown in the footage.
