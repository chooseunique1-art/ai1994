Senior Opposition leaders sharply criticised Prime Minister Narendra Modi for remarks he delivered at Delhi University's Shri Ram College of Commerce during its centenary celebrations. The Prime Minister revived his 'Dimagi Naxal' and 'Naraz Fufa' terminology, claiming his political critiques exposed individuals promoting divisive ideologies in academic and public spheres. Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge accused Modi of using a prestigious academic platform to target political opponents instead of addressing youth concerns. 

Opposition spokespersons argued that this rhetoric creates unnecessary labels to divert attention from economic challenges, education policy, and employment needs, leading various parties to issue formal statements condemning the tone. Ruling party leaders defended the speech, stating that highlighting ideological subversion is crucial for national progress and security. Student organizations and educational policy experts expressed mixed views on political discourse in universities and maintaining spaces focused on research and growth.
