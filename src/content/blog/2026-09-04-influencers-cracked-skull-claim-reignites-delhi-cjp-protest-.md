A political controversy has flared in Delhi over an assault allegation dating back to a Cockroach Janta Party demonstration at Jantar Mantar in July 2026. Activist Nishu Aazad alleged that her father was assaulted during the agitation by social media influencer Swatantra Bhardwaj, resulting in a serious head injury. Delhi Police stated that the injury came from a scuffle involving a metal bangle, describing the wound as simple rather than life-threatening, while Bhardwaj maintained he acted in self-defense.

The dispute escalated when Bhardwaj boasted on a podcast that he had cracked the skull of the protesting student's father. This remark spread online and drew condemnation from activists and opposition figures, who seized on the comment as evidence of impunity. Conversely, Bhardwaj's supporters pointed to the police assessment of a minor injury as proof that the claim was exaggerated.

Aazad separately accused the police of failing to register a formal case, but police pushed back by stating that an FIR under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita was registered at the Parliament Street police station following her statement. The episode has become a flashpoint regarding how Delhi Police handle protesters and influencers, with local television channels and political commentators picking up the story to call for clarity and accountability.
