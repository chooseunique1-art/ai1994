An Iranian deputy foreign minister, Kazem Gharibabadi, suggested that Donald Trump suffers from schizophrenia. The comment followed a post by Trump on Truth Social claiming that the Strait of Hormuz is a US territory.

Gharibabadi wrote on X that a common symptom of the condition involves hallucinations and delusions. He added that psychologists recommend patients with this condition receive treatment and remain under 24-hour supervision.
