At the 2026 Asian Games, the skeet shooting competition delivered two bronze medals for the participating team.  Both shooters performed consistently enough to secure the third‑place finish in their respective events.

Meanwhile, Indian star Manu Bhaker competed in the shooting program but was unable to reach the podium.  Her performance fell short of the scores needed for a medal in her discipline.

The results highlight a mixed outcome for the contingent, with team success in skeet contrasting with an individual miss for Bhaker.
