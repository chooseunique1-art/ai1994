On August 15, 2026, a video shared by UAE_BARQ and credited to TRT Haber showed a shopping mall ceiling collapsing in China after rainwater leaked through the roof. Footage captured water and debris crashing into the mall's interior near a walkway shortly before the collapse occurred. 

The post noted that the mall was closed following the incident and work in the affected area was halted while repairs are assessed. No casualties were reported, and the specific city and mall name were not provided in the post or visible in the video captions.
