US President Donald Trump has suggested renaming the Strait of Hormuz "Trump Strait" after posting on Truth Social that the United States has the strategic waterway "under control." Trump previously relabelled the Gulf of Mexico as the "Gulf of America" and proposed renaming Lake Ontario as "Lake America," though neither proposal was accepted by Mexico or Canada. He has also circulated maps depicting the strait as new US territory, an assertion without recognized standing in international law.

The claim follows months of disruption in the strait by Iran amid a wider war, which has forced energy exporters like Qatar and the UAE to use costly workarounds such as ship-to-ship cargo transfers. While Trump's comments drew social media attention, no formal reaction had been reported from Iran or regional governments. Analysts noted that his rhetorical claims do not reflect the reality on the ground, where shipping remains severely constrained.

The renaming proposal is symbolic and carries no legal weight without unlikely international recognition, given Iran's continued contestation of the waterway. However, the episode highlights how central control of Hormuz has become to Trump's public messaging during a time of elevated oil prices and market monitoring of the region.
