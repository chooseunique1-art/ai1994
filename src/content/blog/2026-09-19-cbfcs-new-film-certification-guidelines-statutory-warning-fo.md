The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting notified revised film certification guidelines under the Cinematograph Act on September 16, 2026. These guidelines include a statutory warning for scenes depicting drug use. 

This developing story has drawn significant attention from political analysts, policy makers, and the general public. Stakeholders are engaging with authorities to evaluate potential impacts, while officials highlight the need to adhere to established protocols during administrative reviews.
