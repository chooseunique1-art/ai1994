Actor-politician C. Joseph Vijay, chief of the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam, attacked the ruling DMK during Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly proceedings on September 7, 2026. Citing Enforcement Directorate documents on the TASMAC scam and the Sarkaria Commission, Vijay accused senior DMK leaders of systematic corruption and condemned the party's historic lifting of liquor prohibitions, stating it has devastated working-class families. His speech prompted counter-protests and a walkout by DMK legislators.

Vijay also warned against misogynistic comments and double entendres directed at women in public life, specifically referencing online harassment of female political figures and actors like Trisha. Emphasizing that women voters form the backbone of his party's support base, he declared that disrespect towards women is strictly intolerable. He stated his party aims to present an alternative governance model free from regional dynasty politics and corruption.

In response, DMK leaders dismissed Vijay's claims as publicity stunts for political posturing ahead of upcoming elections. Observers noted that the Assembly clash marks a significant escalation as new political entities challenge traditional Dravidian party dominance, igniting widespread commentary across southern India.
