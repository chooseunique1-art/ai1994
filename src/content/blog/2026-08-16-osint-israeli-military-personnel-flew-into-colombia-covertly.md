On August 14, 2026, the OSINT account MenchOsint published flight-tracking data and images alleging that Israeli army soldiers and officers covertly arrived in Colombia on a Boeing 707 "Re'em" tanker aircraft with tail number 295. According to the post, the aircraft flew from the Middle East with its identification markings removed and its transponder switched off. The account also claimed in a quote-tweet that Morocco permitted the Israeli Air Force aircraft to fly through its airspace en route to the United States after Spain refused the request.

Officials from Israel, Colombia, and Morocco have not confirmed either claim, and mainstream outlets have not independently verified the Colombia flight. The OSINT account regularly shares flight and military data, and these reports remain unverified.
