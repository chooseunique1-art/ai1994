Senior Indian National Congress leader and former Union Minister E.M. Sudarsana Natchiappan has passed away at the age of 78 after a brief illness. Hailing from Tamil Nadu, he served multiple terms in the Rajya Sabha, held key ministerial portfolios, and was widely respected across party lines for his contributions to parliamentary democracy and legislative reforms. 

Party leaders from various political spectrums have expressed deep condolences, remembering his commitment to public service, constitutional values, grassroots governance, social justice, and institutional transparency. Family members, political associates, and local citizens have gathered to pay their last respects to the veteran leader.

His mortal remains are expected to be taken to his native district in Tamil Nadu for state honors and final rites. Political analysts note that his departure leaves a noticeable void in regional and national political discourse, particularly regarding legal acumen within party structures.
