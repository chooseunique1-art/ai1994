title: "Brief outage hits regional satellite navigation signal"
description: "Operator says the disruption lasted under two hours and traced it to a software update."
pubDate: 2026-08-18

A satellite navigation provider confirmed a signal disruption lasting roughly
90 minutes, tracing the cause to a software update pushed to ground control
systems.

No related safety incidents have been reported. The operator said a rollback
was completed and normal service resumed by early evening.

This item has no linked source page; the summary above reflects the operator's
public statement in full.
