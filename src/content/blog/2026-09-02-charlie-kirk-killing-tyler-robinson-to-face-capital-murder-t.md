A Utah judge ruled that Tyler Robinson will stand trial on capital murder charges for the fatal shooting of conservative activist Charlie Kirk. The ruling follows a preliminary hearing where prosecutors presented evidence linking Robinson to the shooting, and the judge found sufficient grounds for a full trial. Robinson has pleaded not guilty.

If convicted, prosecutors intend to seek the death penalty, and the case has drawn intense national attention. Pretrial proceedings are expected to continue for months before a trial date is set, while Robinson's defense team has not yet detailed its trial strategy. 

Kirk's death prompted widespread reaction across the political spectrum, renewed debate over political violence in the United States, and left a void in conservative youth political organizing. While court proceedings have included detailed accounts of investigator evidence, many specifics remain under seal or subject to pretrial motions.
