Tom Barrack, the US Special Presidential Envoy for Syria and Iraq, called Israeli airstrikes on the Abu al-Duhur military airbase in Idlib an "unnecessary escalation" that does not advance regional stability. The strikes hit the base's runway on August 18, 2026, resulting in material damage to infrastructure but no casualties, according to Syrian state TV. 

Barrack, who is also the US ambassador to Turkey, stated that Syria's interim government under President Ahmed al-Sharaa has not adopted a predatory posture or maintained proxy forces, and has indicated a preference for de-escalation with Israel. He urged all parties to prioritize logical discourse and avoid further military incidents, while the Israeli military offered no immediate comment on his remarks.
