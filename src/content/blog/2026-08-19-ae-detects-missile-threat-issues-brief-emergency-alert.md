On the evening of August 18, 2026, UAE authorities issued an emergency alert warning residents of a missile threat detected by the country's air defence systems. The National Emergency Crisis and Disaster Management Authority advised residents to seek a safe place in the nearest secure building, though the alert did not specify the origin of the threat. 

The Ministry of Defence stated on X that any sounds heard were the result of air defence interceptions and affirmed its full readiness to respond to any threat and safeguard the UAE's sovereignty, security, and stability. Authorities confirmed that air defence systems were actively responding.

The alert was lifted shortly after with no reported casualties or damage. The Ministry of Defence shared the post in Arabic and English via its official channels, and no further details on the source of the threat were provided at the time of writing.
