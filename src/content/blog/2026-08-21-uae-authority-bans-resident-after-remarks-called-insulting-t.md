On August 20, 2026, the UAE's National Media Authority announced legal action against a female resident after an online video showed her making disparaging comments implying a "scarcity of natural forms" among Emirati girls. The remarks sparked widespread backlash on social media, with commentators condemning the statements as a dismissal of Emirati women's identity and stating that the dignity of Emirati women is a "red line." 

The National Media Authority confirmed the legal measures under the hashtag #عميد_الإمارات. Under UAE law, online insults and defamation are treated as serious offenses, and media regulations prohibit content that violates respect for national identity and citizens' dignity. 

As of the report, the exact penalty imposed on the resident, including any fine or broadcasting ban, had not been made public.
