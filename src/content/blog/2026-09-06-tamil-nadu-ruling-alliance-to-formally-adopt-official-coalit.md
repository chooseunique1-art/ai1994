Tamil Nadu Minister Dr. Arunraj announced that the state's ruling political alliance will formally adopt its official coalition name on September 9. The decision follows high-level consultative meetings where ministers met with key partner representatives, including TNCC President B. Manickam Tagore and VCK Chief Thol. Thirumavalavan. 

Minister Arunraj confirmed that the title will be finalized after extensive internal deliberations with all partner entities and public feedback. The coalition includes the ruling party, Congress, VCK, Left parties, and IUML. Party delegates meeting in Chennai emphasized that a unified identity will help streamline administrative messaging and grassroots coordination.

Further details regarding joint committee formations and policy manifestos are expected to be unveiled alongside the new title. The move aims to institutionalize the ruling block as the government completes its initial administrative rollout following recent assembly victories.
